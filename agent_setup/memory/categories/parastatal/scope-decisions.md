---
title: Parastatal scope decisions (research team - applies to all cities)
---
Until the team decides an item, treat it as NEEDS REVIEW.
- Companies fully owned by the city government (ULG SPVs, e.g. a city bus company):
  TEAM TO DECIDE (count as parastatal / not).
- Public transport bodies that are building and will operate, not yet open:
  eligible with PRE-OPERATIONAL flag (per v2 category 3), unless the team decides otherwise.
- Small special-area development authorities (e.g. carved out of a larger UDA, powers
  entrusted to a company): TEAM TO DECIDE.
- State-wide bodies with only a partial role in the city (e.g. peri-urban villages):
  TEAM TO DECIDE.
