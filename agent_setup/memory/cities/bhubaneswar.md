Guidance, not evidence. Taken from the first Bhubaneswar Sources workbook (16 Nov 2026). Nothing below has been confirmed from an official text yet; every line is to be confirmed by the team.
- City government: Bhubaneswar Municipal Corporation, under the Orissa Municipal Corporation Act, 2003; to be confirmed by the team (the register's name for it was added by hand and has not been checked against an official source).
- BDA (Bhubaneswar Development Authority): governed by the Odisha Development Authorities Act, 1982; to be confirmed by the team. Its documents are on bda.gov.in, cms.bhubaneswarone.in (annual reports, rules, regulations) and lfaodisha.nic.in (Local Fund Audit reports); to be confirmed by the team.
- WATCO (Water Corporation of Odisha): a company under the Companies Act, 2013, incorporated 24 Nov 2015, so it may have no parent Act: check its Memorandum and Articles of Association and the government orders that set it up; to be confirmed by the team.
- WATCO website: watcoodisha.nic.in (the candidate watcoodisha.in has also been seen); which is official is to be confirmed by the team.
- Odisha Water Works Rules (the 1980 Rules, amended in 2021 by a Housing and Urban Development Department resolution in the Extraordinary Gazette of 24 Nov 2021, which names WATCO) are on sujog.odisha.gov.in; to be confirmed by the team.
- CRUT (Comprehensive Region Urban Transport, formerly Capital Region Urban Transport): appears to be a special purpose vehicle under the Housing and Urban Development Department; its legal form and constituting instrument are to be confirmed by the team.
- CRUT websites: comprehensiveregiontransport.in and capitalregiontransport.in (the name changed, so both appear); to be confirmed by the team. State pages that mention it: odishatransport.gov.in and urban.odisha.gov.in; to be confirmed by the team.
- Other Odisha state pages that may help: rtiodisha.gov.in (RTI manuals of each office) and odisha.gov.in; to be confirmed by the team.
