title: Bengaluru - city context (all verticals)
---
- City government: prior project notes say the Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024 replaced
  BBMP with the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) and City Corporations. Treat every date,
  member list and relationship about this transition as UNVERIFIED until an official source
  fetched in the run confirms it.
- Known conflict: prior drafts give two different effective dates (15 May 2025 and
  2 Sept 2025). Reuse neither. Establish the date from an official source, or write
  "Needs team confirmation".
- Eras for governance sources: GBA-era | BBMP-era | Pre-BBMP | Transitional | n/a. A BBMP-era
  statement is never evidence of the current arrangement.
- Laws may still name "Corporation of the City of Bangalore" or BBMP. Record the wording
  exactly, and whether an official source shows a successor body substituted; if not
  verified -> CONFIRM.
- Collect the GBA Act as the city government's law: functions schedules, planning-authority
  provisions, and any provisions giving it coordination, supervision, direction or approval
  powers over public authorities. Record section numbers as location notes only.
- Useful official places to search (all must still pass the source rules): state legislation
  portal (consolidated/amended Acts and Rules); eGazette; state legislature papers-laid pages
  (annual reports are often tabled there); DPAR (appointment/transfer orders); the state
  service-guarantee portal (Sakala); the state public-procurement portal; the state RTI online
  portal; CAG.
