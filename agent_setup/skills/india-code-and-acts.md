# Reading Indian Acts and India Code

- An Act's operative provisions are in **numbered sections** (e.g. "Section 16(2)"). Cite the
  section number with every legal claim.
- **"shall" / "must"** creates a mandatory duty; **"may"** gives discretion. Methodologies
  often score these differently, so quote the exact verb.
- Check for **amendments**: the current text may differ from the original Act. Note the year
  of the version you rely on.
- **India Code** (indiacode.nic.in) handle pages often show only metadata (title, year, type)
  and not the text of the Act. Such a page is NOT the source of a provision. Look for the full
  text: the PDF linked from India Code, the state law department or gazette, or the
  department's "Acts & Rules" page.
- State Acts are often published by the state law / legislative department and the
  administering department, as scanned or text PDFs. A scanned PDF without readable text
  cannot be verified automatically.
- Rules, regulations and government orders made under an Act can be binding; name the
  instrument and its date.
