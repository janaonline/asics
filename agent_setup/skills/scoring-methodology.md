# Applying the ASICS methodology

The question's **Detailed Methodology is the rule.** This file, the section skills and team memory
are help only. If they disagree with the methodology, follow the methodology and add
`CONFIRM: <file> says X, the methodology says Y`.

Each question has an **Assessment Level**:

- **Law/Policy**: what the formal framework (law or policy) intends, enables or mandates.
  Look for binding provisions and distinguish *shall/must* from *may*.
- **Implementation**: whether mandated processes actually operate in practice. Look for
  current, dated evidence of practice, not provisions in law. A legal mandate alone is not
  evidence of implementation.

When scoring:
- Follow the question's **Detailed Methodology** exactly; propose a score only when it gives a
  rule and the evidence supports it.
- Do not credit generic phrases ("public interest", "inclusive development", "all
  consumers") unless the source turns them into a specific provision.
- Law/Policy questions: use the version in force on the cut-off date (the app gives only such
  sources) and say which. Implementation questions: prefer the most recent document and say
  which year it covers; an old document is at most partial evidence of current practice.
- If the evidence answers only part of the question, say which part is missing.

# Rules that apply to every category and every city (v2)
- A rubric 0 (or a "NO" input that scores 0) needs AFFIRMATIVE ABSENCE: every relevant
  instrument or page was in the Citation Sheet and read, nothing was found, and you list what
  you checked. Otherwise the result is "not found": leave the score/input blank.
- Scale mismatch: if the highest rubric band is not the Max Score and not a sum of components,
  do not score; record the band and raw value with "SCALE-MISMATCH" and ask for confirmation.
  Exception, scoring on the older workbook path only: when the task input has a SCALE line,
  decide the band, then enter band x Max Score / top band, as it says. In the team's
  templates the task gives ALLOWED SCORES and nothing is converted.
- Rubric gap: if the evidence fits no band, do not create or interpolate a band; leave the
  score blank and ask for confirmation.
- Website evidence that could not be opened automatically is a verification limit, never a
  negative finding: never score it 0.
- The question-bank's ID-specific anomalies are in the section guidance for that question.
- ASICS Score, the Citation Sheet's Authority Score, Class (A1/A2/R/P/M) and Confidence are
  separate; none is derived from another.
