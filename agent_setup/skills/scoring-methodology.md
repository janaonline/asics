# Applying the ASICS methodology

Each question has an **Assessment Level**:

- **Law/Policy**: what the formal framework (law or policy) intends, enables or mandates.
  Look for binding provisions and distinguish *shall/must* from *may*.
- **Institutional Design**: how institutions are formally structured and relate to one
  another (functions, jurisdiction, board composition, reporting lines, appointments,
  decision rights). An Act may be the source.
- **Implementation**: whether mandated processes actually operate in practice. Look for
  current, dated evidence of practice, not provisions in law. A legal mandate alone is not
  evidence of implementation.

When scoring:
- Follow the question's **Detailed Methodology** exactly; propose a score only when it gives a
  rule and the evidence supports it.
- Do not credit generic phrases ("public interest", "inclusive development", "all
  consumers") unless the source turns them into a specific provision.
- Prefer the most recent document; say which year it covers. An old document is at most
  partial evidence of current practice.
- If the evidence answers only part of the question, say which part is missing.
