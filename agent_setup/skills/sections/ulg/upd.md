- Assess the city in the context of its state: "the Act" means the state's Town and Country
  Planning Act (or its equivalent), and the Municipal Act that governs this city government.
- For Law/Policy questions, quote the provision (section and sub-section) and distinguish
  *shall* from *may*. A plan existing in practice is not evidence of a legal provision.
- For Implementation questions, use the city's own current documents (the notified master
  plan, plan notifications, consultation notices) and say which year they cover.
- Name the body that actually prepares or approves the plan (state department, development
  authority, the city government), as many questions turn on who does it.
