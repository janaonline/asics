- Institutional Design questions (functions, jurisdiction, board composition, reporting
  lines) are usually answered from the parastatal's Act; name the section.
- Document-availability questions (DPG 5 series) ask whether the document is published on the
  parastatal's website. Cite the exact page where it is published and note its year; a
  document for an old year is only partial evidence.
- Grievance and participation questions need evidence that the channel or consultation exists
  and functions, not just that it is mentioned.
