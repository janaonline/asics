title: State Capacities
evidence_hints: revenue expenditure capital budget estimates actuals audited accounts own revenue grants staff sanctioned working filled women tenure term removal managing director chairman appointment transfer order
---
- Numeric questions: state every input figure, its financial year, units and the page or
  table it comes from, then show the formula. Round only the final score, to 2 decimals.
  Use audited accounts over budget estimates where the methodology allows.
  - SC-1 = own revenue (tax + non-tax; excluding all government grants and transfers; loans
    are not revenue) / total expenditure x 100, averaged over 2-5 completed FYs (prefer the
    latest 3); score = % / 10, capped at 10.
  - SC-3 = |actual - budgeted expenditure| / budgeted x 100 per FY, averaged over >= 2 FYs;
    <= 15% -> 10, > 15% -> 0; add "CONFIRM: variance definition".
  - SC-2: absolute figure only; score null; CONFIRM (needs a peer rank).
  - SC-7a = filled / sanctioned; SC-7b = women / total filled.
- Executive authority (SC-5, SC-6): identify the post by FUNCTION. In some boards the
  whole-time Chairman is the executive head, so "not the Chairperson or political head"
  excludes a political or part-time chair, not a whole-time executive chair. If ambiguous,
  CONFIRM.
- Read the Rules (SC-4a, SC-5, SC-6): an Act may set no tenure while the Rules made under it
  fix a term. Report every layer, then apply the SC-5 band rule (a fixed term is not a
  minimum-tenure guarantee without removal protection).
- SC-6: count distinct substantive holders of the executive post in the last 5 years from
  official orders only; list acting/additional/concurrent-charge holders separately and do not
  count them.
- Tenure and staffing questions need dated, official records (orders, annual reports,
  sanctioned vs working strength tables).

- Question-bank anomalies (Parastatal question bank):
  - SC-5: 10 needs minimum tenure >= 2 years AND removal only on defined grounds; 5 needs
    minimum tenure < 2 years. A fixed term (e.g. "holds office for 3 years") with removal at
    government discretion fits neither band: record term, removal rule and source; score
    null; CONFIRM.
  - SC-7a gives 0 for "no staffing data": not found is not absent -> Class R; score null.
  - SC-6 evidence text names media reports: cite official orders only; if only media evidence
    would exist, say so; Class at most A2, Confidence Low, score null.
