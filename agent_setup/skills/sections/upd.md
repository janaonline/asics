- This file is help only. The question's Detailed Methodology is the rule: if this file and the
  methodology differ, follow the methodology and add "CONFIRM: <this file> says X, the
  methodology says Y".
- For Law/Policy questions, quote the operative provision of the parastatal's Act, its Rules,
  or the Town and Country Planning Act, and state whether it uses "shall" or "may".
- For Implementation questions, rely on the latest plan document, consultation record or
  minutes, and give its year. A legal mandate alone is not evidence of practice.
- Do not credit generic phrases such as "public interest" or "inclusive development" unless
  the source translates them into a specific provision.
- UPD 1a, 1b and 2a-2d are relationship questions: read the parastatal side AND the ULG side
  (ULG Act, T&CP Act). Class A2 unless one provision states the relationship explicitly.
- Partial-scope provisions (pattern from the pilot AI-vs-human audit): a development authority may
  have no general duty to plan with the ULG, but specific provisions may require it to notify
  the ULG of development schemes, allow representations, or obtain ULG concurrence for some
  layouts. Report EACH mechanism with the plan type it covers (master plan vs development
  scheme vs layout) and its legal strength. If the band depends on whether scheme- or
  layout-level mechanisms count as "spatial and/or sectoral plans": score null +
  "CONFIRM: plan-scope". A T&CP public notice to all citizens is not ULG coordination.
- Advisory bodies: a statutory consultative/advisory committee with ULG or citizen members is
  "consultation", not approval. Report its composition, what it advises on, and whether its
  advice must be considered or answered.
- UPD 2: each parastatal can take only some of the SQs; the MQ maximum is the sum of the
  SQs that apply to it.
- UPD 1a / UPD 1b: the methodology's bands run to 10 but the Max Score is 5. Apply the
  methodology: record the band it gives and add "CONFIRM: scale". Do not blank the score for this
  reason alone.
