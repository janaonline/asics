# Required sources: decisions for the programme team

The table `data/question_source_requirements.yaml` says which documents a question needs before the AI may give a score of 0. It is a **draft**: it works with the defaults below even if nobody answers. Where I was unsure, I made the row ask for more, so the worst case is a blank score, not a wrong 0.

Each question has a recommended default. Answer "Yes" or "No" (or write a change). Counts come from a free test on the current Bengaluru and Bhubaneswar Citation Sheets, using the 125 rows where people gave 0 and the AI left the score blank. They show the effect of the default, not whether a 0 is right.

Tags such as [ACT] or [RULES] are the first word of "What This Source Is Useful For" in the Citation Sheet. If the team edits a row, keep its tag.

## What I changed in the table for you to see

- **Needs a decision (19 of 52 rows):** the "Check ..." sentence and the Evidence cell named different sources, or the Evidence cell is empty. The row uses both (the stricter). Search `needs_decision: true` in the table.
- **Source tags used:** [ACT], [RULES], [TCP] (Town and Country Planning Act), [ULG-LAW] (State Municipal Act), [BUDGET], [ACCOUNTS], [AR] (annual report), [PLANS] or [SECTORPLAN], [TARIFF], [CE-APPT], [BOARD-LIST], [WEB] (official website) and the new [CONSTITUTION].
