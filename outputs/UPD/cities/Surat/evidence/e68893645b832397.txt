21. Revision of development plan 
 
 Atleast once in ten years from the date on 
which a final development plan comes into 
force, the area development authority shall 
revise the development plan after carrying out, 
if necessary, a fresh survey and the provisions 
of sections 9 to 20, shall, so far as may be, 
apply to such revision.
