Commuter Advisory and Facilitation Committee (CAFC) 
         Commuter Advisory and Facilitation Committee (CAFC) was formed in the year 2005. 
        BMTC Commuter Advisory and Facilitation Committee comprises of the representatives 
from the management of BMTC and representatives from the Public.  
       The Chairman of the committee shall formulate the plan of action to improve the services to 
the commuters by conducting meetings of the committee once in three months. The decisions 
taken during such meetin gs shall be implemented in letter and spirit so as to improve the 
facilities to the travelling public as well as performance of the organization.

The committee may interact with Commuters, NGO’s, Residents Associations and other 
agencies. The committee shall meet once in three months or more frequently if so requested by 
the managing Director.  
         The committee will cover issues such as; 
a. Feed back mechanism, corrective actions, rapport between service provider (BMTC) and 
the travelling public.  
b. Citizens charter, additional support through public participation. 
c. Resolution of problems/grievance of Commuters, Employees and Citizens.  
d. Sharing of information. 
e. Support for new ventures and formulation of ideas in innovative and creati ve aspects 
aiming at improvement in the services, and  
f. Any other issues at the request of the MD, BMTC. 
The committee shall submit its report from time to time to the Managing Director and will be in 
force until further orders.
