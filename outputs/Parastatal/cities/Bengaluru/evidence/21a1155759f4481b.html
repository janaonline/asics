Online RTI Information System
 

Please do not file RTI application/first appeal through this portal for Departments/Public Authorities under Central Government or other State Governments. If filed, the application would be returned without refund of the fee amount.

This is a portal to file RTI application/first appeal online along with payment gateway. Fee payment can be made through Internet Banking. Through this portal RTI application/first appeal can be filed by Indian Citizen only for the Departments/Public Authorities of the Govt. of Karnataka
Please read instructions carefully while submitting the RTI application/first appeal.



DISCLAIMER

  1. Department of Citizen Services is the nodal agency for managing the RTI Online application portal. The respective Public Authorities which receives the RTI Online applications and first appeals are responsible for disposal of RTI applications and first appeals received online through the portal.
  2. With reference to point 1, only the issues regarding RTI Online portal, may be addressed to the RTI Online PMU of Department of Citizen Services. Any complaints with regard to RTI applications, delays in disposal in stipulated time needs to be submitted to the Nodal Officers of respective Public Authorities only. The Public Authority wise Nodal Officers list is available in the RTI Online portal.
  3. The RTI ONLINE Application/Appeal does not have the provision to transfer an application online, under Section 6 (3) of the RTI Act to any Public Authority where-in RTI ONLINE facility is still not on-boarded in the portal.
  4. The RTI online application/First appeal can be filed only for the public authorities which are listed in the Nodal Officers menu of the RTI online portal. If RTI application/First appeal needs to be submitted to the public authority which are not listed in this portal, it is recommended to file RTI Application in Physical Form (Hard Copy) to such Public Authorities.
  5. Do not Upload UID/Aadhaar Card/Pan Card/Passport/Personal Data.
