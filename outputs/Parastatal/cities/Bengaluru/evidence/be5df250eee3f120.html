Change in ownership of property having BWSSB Water Supply and Sanitary / only Water Supply /only Sanitary connection will have to reflect the change of ownership against the connection number(RRNUMBER) alloted by the Bangalore Water Supply and Sewrage Board. In order to obtain the service, the consumer requires to apply online through this site by way of paying a nominal fee of Rs.250/-.

Note: When applying for Name Change/Ownership Change, please use this portal only if there is no additional construction or reconstruction since the sanctioning of Water and Sanitary connection from BWSSB to the premises. If any additional construction or reconstruction has taken place in the premises, kindly apply through Online Water Connection - Jaladhare portal using URL https://owc.bwssb.gov.in/
