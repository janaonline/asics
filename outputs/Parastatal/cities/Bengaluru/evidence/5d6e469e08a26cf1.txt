INTRODUCTION

Preamble:
Before bifurcation from KSRTC, there were two Divisions BTS South and BTS North headed by Director since 1993. Since 1961 it was under MSRTC/KSRTC. Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation (BMTC) was formed as an independent Corporation with effect from 15-08-1997 after bifurcation from Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation, vide GO No. HTD/127/ TRA/ 96 dated 07-8-1997.

Consequent upon the formation of BMTC, the organization was structured to function under two-tier system viz., Depots & Corporate office. During the year 2011-12 the administration system was switched over to three tier structure viz, Depots, Divisions and Corporate Office with a view to have closer liaison and better control. Again in 2017-18 administration switched over to two tier system for better Management, Operations and Maintenance.

Management:
The amended KSRTC Act 1982 provides for management of the Corporation by Board of Directors. As on 31st March 2025 the Board of Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation consisted of 10 Directors including Hon'ble Chairman (Non-Official). Apart from Hon'ble Chairman the remaining 9 Directors are Official Directors. All the Directors are appointed by the State Government.

Administrative Setup:
The Corporation is functioning under two-tier administration system with corporate office and depots. There were 50 depots functioning under 7 Zones, 4 Central Workshops and fully equipped & modernized Driving Training Centre at Vaddarahalli under its jurisdiction as on 31-03-2025. The zone wise details of depots are given in Chapter-2.

Growth during 2024-25:
BMTC is catering to the transport services in city and suburban areas of Bengaluru in a radius of about 40.4 kms and the area of operation is expanded from 3527 Sq. kms to 5130 Sq. kms in view of Greater Bengaluru. The number of Schedules operated as on 31-03-2025 was 5871. During the year 2024-25, new depot has started functioning at Uttanahalli. During the year, 716 Non A/C 12 meter electric buses are added to the fleet on GCC Model by Tata Smart City Mobility Solutions Limited and 58 A/C 12 meter electric buses are introduced by entering into agreement with M/s OHM Global Mobility Ltd. As a part of Government of India initiative to reduce pollution, 820 new fully built Ashok Leyland BS-VI diesel buses are inducted to the fleet under Government of Karnataka grants. During the year, 709 aged buses have been scrapped which were not fit for operation and 115 buses have been sold to KSRTC & other Educational Institutions. Thus, totally 824 vehicles have been removed from fleet and total fleet strength was 6872 at the year end.

Operational Efficiency:
The operational results of the year 2024-25 are as under:

a. Schedules and Vehicles: As at the end of 2024-25, 5871 schedules are operated utilizing 6872 vehicles. Average schedules operated during the year is 5489.8.

b. Average Effective Kms. operated per day: During the year under report 11.34 lakh effective kms. were operated on an average.

c. Average Vehicle Utilization: The average vehicle utilization performed during the year under report is 206.7 kms.

d. KMPL (Diesel): The performance of diesel KMPL has been recorded at 3.75 during the year under report.

e. Rate of Breakdowns per 10,000 kms: During the year 2024-25 breakdowns rate of 0.02 per 10,000 kms. has been recorded.

f. Rate of Accidents per lakh kilometers: Accidents rate of 0.05 per 1 lakh kms. has been recorded during the year.

Financial Performance:
1. Revenue: Corporation has realized traffic revenue of Rs. 2039.09 crore realizing average traffic revenue of Rs 558.65 lakhs per day. Launching of Shakthi Yojane in June 2023 has led to considerable increase in traffic revenue realization of the Corporation. Further, commercial revenue of Rs.345.67 crore has been earned from other sources. The Government has provided salary & working capital grants of Rs.155.11 crore other than concessional/free passes reimbursement of Rs.228.39 crore. Thus, the gross revenue earning of the Corporation during the year under report is Rs.2768.27 crore.

2. Cost of Operations: The Corporation has incurred total expenditure of Rs.3438.75 crore towards the cost of operations comprising of variable and fixed costs.

3. Margin: The Corporation sustained a loss of Rs.670.48 crore on gross revenue during the year under report. The reasons for loss are as under:
• Traffic revenue realization not up to the mark due to shifting of BMTC bus passengers to Namma Metro and app based Cab/Bike services such as Ola/Uber/Rapido, etc.
• Some of the citizens of Bengaluru opting for personalized mode of transport such as bikes & cars.
• Deficit in various grants to be released by Government of Karnataka including Shakthi Yojane grants.

4. Passenger amenities and facilities:
The Corporation has accorded high priority for providing increased amenities to the commuters. Three major Bus Stations at Subhashnagar (Kempegowda Bus Station), Shivajinagar

a. Fool Proof Defects Management system
b. Launching of Mobile app to record the defects of vehicles by drivers
c. Zero avoidable out breakdowns
d. Rationalization of schedules based on Real Time operation data from ITS

f. Public relations for Effective Transport Management Initiatives
g. Innovative Technology for Effective Transport Management Initiatives
h. Vehicular Air pollution Management Initiatives
i. Road Safety Management Initiatives

Programme for 2025-26
The Corporation has formulated an Action Plan for 2025-26 and steps are taken to improve operational efficiency by setting norms not only to bring down the cost of operations but also to improve various operational parameters. The details are as under:

• It is expected that the Corporation may incur Rs.4379.03 Cost and CPKM of 9213.0 paise.
• It is anticipated that the Corporation will sustain a loss of Rs.907.15 crore on Gross Revenue.

3.6 Services operated:

• NICE Road Service:
For the convenience of commuters from the outskirts of Bangalore, a NICE Road route is being implemented from various important places to Electronic City Wipro Gate. Currently, BMTC is operating 55 NICE road schedules with 286 trips from various parts of the city to Electronic City Wipro gate.

• Metro Feeder Services:
The Corporation is operating Metro Feeder services to provide first and last mile connectivity for Metro Rail commuters in the city. These buses are specially designed and flexible enough to be operated through narrow areas. It is planned to expand network of these services with the expansion of Metro.

• Wonder-la Service:
BMTC is operating buses from various parts of the city Wonder-la Park with 10 to 20 trips daily. Additional trips will also be operated on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays depending on the passenger demand. Wonder-Law is offering a 15% discount on the price of tickets for every passenger who purchases a ticket and travels in BMTC air-conditioned Vajra services.

• Bengaluru Rounds:

BMTC being the sole Public Transport provider in Bengaluru is fulfilling many social obligations in the interest of the commuters and general public. Many of these schemes are unique to BMTC. ii) Medical Reimbursement: Every valid Monthly Commuter pass holder is provided with a Medical reimbursement facility upto a maximum of Rs. 30,000/- towards in-patient medical expenses in case of injury from vehicular accidents. The payment will be made to the commuter on production of the necessary bills/vouchers, after due verification.

2. Twin Benefit Scheme for Vajra Gold Monthly Pass
1) Accident Insurance Coverage to the tune of Rs. 5 Lakh in case of death or permanent incapacitation resulting from any vehicular accident.

3. Twin Benefit Scheme for Vayu Vajra Gold Monthly Pass
1) Accident Insurance Coverage to the tune of Rs. 10 Lakh in case of death or permanent incapacitation resulting from any vehicular accident.
2) Medical Re-imbursement upto a maximum of Rs. 50,000/- in case of Accidental injuries necessitating inpatient treatment.

4. Twin Benefit scheme for Student Concessional pass holders (w.e.f. July 2004)

Chapter – 10 SECURITY AND VIGILANCE

Bengaluru Metropolitan Transport Corporation's motto is to provide safe and economic transportation to the travelling public. Security and Vigilance Department is functioning as "Eyes and Ears" of the Corporation.

The main objective of Security and Vigilance department is to safeguard the Corporation property from theft, fire, etc. by taking precautionary measures. It also conducts preventive checks on HSD, stores, cash, tickets and various types of passes handled and maintained by depots/workshops/bus stations and Central Office to ensure proper accountability and reports on irregularities if any.

The Security & Vigilance department keeps an eye on instructions issued by BMTC through various orders/circulars/General Standing Orders, etc. If any deviations/ omissions committed by officers/officials in their discharge of duties, any involvement in corrupt practices, anti-management activities, misappropriations, frauds, etc., reports would be sent to the Disciplinary Authorities for necessary action.

The department deputes line checking staff in different shifts to check the vehicles operated across the city and suburban areas to curb the pilferage and increase the traffic revenue. Further, Sarathi personnel's are also being deputed to ensure traffic rules and to inculcate discipline among the crew.

The department gathers information on the union activities conducted by various labour unions and provides the information to help the management to take preventive measures. Also, the department collects confidential information regarding the sabotage activities and anti-management activities planned by the different labour unions to take appropriate preventive measures.

Chapter – 13 ENVIRONMENT

13.1 Introduction:

BMTC is the largest Urban Public Transport Corporation having a fleet of 6872 buses and carrying about 40.24 lakh passengers every day in Bengaluru City. The population of Bengaluru City is increasing day by day and simultaneously, vehicular population is increasing intensely. Hence, BMTC Environment Cell has been set up during the year 2003 keeping in view responsibility of saving the environment along with operating buses.

13.2 Objectives:

The Environment Cell is constituted with the following primary objectives:

• Achieve good environmental performance in the Corporation
• Establish environmental awareness across the organization
• Afforestation programme at Depots
• Implement new projects on environment as and when necessary
• Reduce air pollution in Bengaluru City on behalf of Corporation

13.3 Environmental Policy:

BMTC being a Government of Karnataka undertaking involved in providing transport solutions to the commuters of Bengaluru city is committed to provide environment friendly operations with the following commitments:

• Motivate and impart training to employees to contribute pro-actively to improve environmental performance
• Adopt environment friendly transport operations through technological up-gradation and pollution reduction measures on continual basis with special focus on natural resources
• Adopt and practice resource optimization measures on continual basis

13.4 Reuse, Recycle and disposal of Hazardous Waste

The oil soaked cotton waste generated from Depots and Workshops will be collected and disposed by M/s Resustainability Industrial Solution Pvt. Ltd., who is Karnataka State Pollution Control Board(KSPCB) authorized agency for recycling the oil soaked cotton waste.

13.5 New Initiatives of Environment Cell:

Handling and Disposal of solid waste like dry, wet and hazardous waste by installing separate dust bins at depots, major Bus Stations, Workshops and TTMCs for different types of waste with neatly labeled and colored code which are placed in easily accessible places. Further, the dry waste is disposed through service provider of BBMP.

13.6 Water Recycling Policy

Water is extensively used in depots for washing and cleaning of buses. The water used for washing and cleaning of buses consists of sludge, oil, grease and impurities. As per the norms prescribed by the KSPCB, the water has to be discharged to the sewage system after treatment. Water recycling plants have been installed in 45 depots to treat and recycle the used water. Water recycle system has been designed & commissioned to recover 95% of used water for reuse, 5% loss is due to runoff, sludge oil and grease etc. The recycled water is being tested once in every six months by authorized agency.

13.7 Water Softening Policy

The hardness of bore water used in depots for washing and cleaning of buses is on very higher side. Hence to reduce the hardness of water, water softener plants have been installed in 45 depots to treat the water and to reduce its hardness. Installation of water softener in remaining depots is under progress.

13.8 Induction of Electric Buses and BS-VI compliant diesel buses in to operation

BMTC started induction of Electric Buses on GCC model and BS-VI compliant diesel buses from 2021-22. At the end of March 2025, 1643 BS-VI diesel buses and 1369 Electric Buses were in operation.

13.9 Smoke Check Drive

• BMTC has the practice of conducting surprise smoke check drive in the buses at depots jointly done with Environment Officer of KSPCB. During smoke check, if any bus is found emitting heavy smoke, necessary work will be carried so that the smoke level of the bus is reduced within the prescribed norms and then only the vehicle will be operated on route.
• At present there are 15 on-line emission testing centers.
