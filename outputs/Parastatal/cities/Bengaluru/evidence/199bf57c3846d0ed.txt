## 
 How to Make BWSSB Bill Payment Online with Karnatakaone?

 Paying your water bill in Bangalore has become more convenient with the KarnatakaOne portal. Through its seamless interface, residents can easily complete their BWSSB bill payment online without the need to visit a physical office.

 Follow the steps below to complete your BWSSB online payment in a few minutes.

 Click Here to Pay Water Bill

 Click Here to Pay Water Bill

## Step 1: Click on the Pay Bill Button 

 Click on the Pay Water Bill Button to open the BWSSB bill payment page. 

## Step 2: Enter Your RR Number

 Locate the "Search For RR Number" field.

 Enter your RR Number as printed on your previous BWSSB water bill.

 Click the green Search button to fetch your billing details.

 This is essential for retrieving your current BWSSB online bill payment information.

## Step 3: Verify Bill and Consumer Details

 Once you search using your RR number, the system will display:

 Consumer Name

 Consumer Address

 Bill Number

 Bill Date

 Bill Amount

 Carefully review these details before proceeding to make your water bill payment.

## Step 4: Enter Guest Mobile Number (Optional)

 If you do not have a KarnatakaOne login, you can proceed as a guest user.

 Enter your mobile number under the Guest Mobile Number section.

 This is optional but recommended for payment confirmations and future communication in case of issues.

 Users with a profile can skip this step and proceed after logging in.

## Step 5: Agree to Terms and Conditions

 Check the box to confirm you agree to the terms and conditions for using online payments.

 This step is required before you can continue with your BWSSB online payment.

## Step 6: Choose a Payment Gateway

 Select your preferred payment method:

 NTT DATA Payment Services

 Razorpay

 Both are secure and support multiple payment options including UPI, debit cards, credit cards, and net banking.

## Step 7: Complete Your BWSSB Water Bill Payment

 Review all the displayed bill details again.

 Choose to either:

 Save the bill to your profile (for registered users), or

 Click Continue for Payment (for both guest and registered users).

 Make the payment using your chosen method.

 Once the transaction is successful, you’ll receive confirmation instantly.

## Having Trouble with BWSSB Online Bill Payment?

 If the transaction fails or is not confirmed:

 Call KarnatakaOne Support: 080-49203888 / 8904085030 (Available 8 AM to 7 PM)

 Or write to: onehelpdesk@karnataka.gov.in

 Support is available to help you reconfirm or troubleshoot any BWSSB bill payment related issues.

## Why Use KarnatakaOne for Water Bill Payment in Bangalore?

 Centralized and secure platform for government bill payments

 Real-time access to BWSSB online bill payment records

 Supports both guest and registered user logins

 Instant digital confirmation and receipts

 Easy access from desktop or mobile browsers
