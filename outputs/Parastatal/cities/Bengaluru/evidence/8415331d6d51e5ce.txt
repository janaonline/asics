 Bus Priority Lanes in Karnataka 

## Cities on the move Challenge – Development of Bus Priority Lanes in Cities:

The Directorate of Urban Land Transport (DULT), being a statewide nodal agency to promote the sustainable transportation modes in the various cities in Karnataka, has come up with the “Cities on the Move Challenge-Where there is a bus, there is a way” to promote the bus priority lanes in the ULBs within Karnataka.
The concerned ULB and the respective public transport agencies hall jointly register and submit a proposal for Bus-Priority Lanes of a minimum of 5 Km of high-frequency bus routes within ULB’s limits.
Best proposals will be funded by DULT and also supported through technical assistance.

## 'Kalyaan Maarg’ - BPL in Kalaburagi city

Implementation of Bus Priority Lane along with Cycle infrastructure under the State Govt. initiative “Cities on Moves challenge- where there is a bus, there is a way” – in Kalaburgi city. 

The objective of the Project:
To develop the comprehensive plan, design for a sustainable and integrated transport system with active mobility.

Project Details:

Total length – 13.68 Km

Component: Bus lane, Bus station, ITS Ducts, Cycle track, Cycle parking Hub, Pigmentation of Cycle track, Footpath Construction, Grill along the corridor on footpath side, , Junction improvement, Relaying of road, Parking bays, median,  Road signages, Road marking, Plater box/ bollard, Cat eyes/ retro-reflecting tape, Gantry, Pigmentation of the cycle track, Bus route map/ Neighborhood map, Wave wall for segregating mixed traffic lane with a cycle lane.

Project Cost:
Rs. 1907.0 Lakhs. The entire project cost is funded by DULT under State Urban Transport Fund. 

Current Status:
Design along with the estimate has been prepared by DULT (In-House).
The tender has been floated and work is awarded to contractors. Work is in progress. 

## BPL in Vijayapura city.

Implementation of Bus Priority Lane along with Cycle infrastructure under the State Govt. “Cities on Moves challenge- where there is a bus, there is a way” – in Vijayapura city. 

Project Details:

Total length – 22.8Km

Component: Bus lane, Bus station, ITS Ducts, Cycle track cum footpath, Pigmentation of Cycle track, Bus route map/ Neighborhood map, Wave wall for segregating mixed traffic lane with a cycle lane.

Project Cost:
Rs. 805.5 Lakhs. To be funded from SUTF by DULT. 

Current Status:
Design along with the estimate is under preparation by DULT (In-House).
