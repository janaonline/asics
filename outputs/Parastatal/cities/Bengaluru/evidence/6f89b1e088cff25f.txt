i) Particulars of the organisation:
ln September 2000, the Govt of Karnataka (GoK) and the Ministry of Railways
(MoR) entered into an MOU to set up a joint venture company called "Rail
lnfrastructure Development Company (Karnataka) Limited - KRIDE'to expedite
development and implementation of identified railway projects in Karnataka.
The Govt of Karnataka incorporated the company under the Companies Act, 1956
at Bangalore on 22nd November 2000 for co-ordinating with local
organisations/Bodies in charge of urban development for the purposeful resolution
of various ancillary matters connected with railway project development. The
Govt of Karnataka through GO No. IDD 59 NSW 2009 dt.17th August, 2009
empowered the company to act as a Nodal Agency for ensuring necessary
co-ordination and smooth implementation of identified railway projects and for
monitoring progress of implementation and report to Govt of Karnataka.
Based on the directives of the Ministry of Railways, the Company was restructured
as a State Joint Venture with equity participation between Government of
Karnataka and Ministry of Railways at 51:49 ratio vide Revised Share Holders
Agreement dated 17.10.2018. ln view of restructuring of K-RIDE as a State JV,
