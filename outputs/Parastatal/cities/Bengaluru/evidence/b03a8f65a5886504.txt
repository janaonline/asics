## BMTC Services available with KarnatakaOne Centres

## Issue of Monthly Pass.

 BMTC serves the citizens of Bangalore with quality bus services.They provide the citizens with exhaustive range of monthly passes and student passes. With a view to provide the citizens of Bangalore an array of options to purchase the bus passes of BMTC, BMTC has integrated their “Issue of Monthly Pass” service for BMTC with KarnatakaOne, so that the citizens can utilize the convenience of purchase of passes at any of the several KarnatakaOne centers spread across Bengaluru city. The mode of payment for buying bus passes, citizen have cash and credit card payment facility. And the rates of Bus passes as per their type are:-. 

 Black Board Pass Rs 725/-
 Sr. Citizen Black Board Pass Rs 650/-
 Pushpak Pass Rs 925/-
 Sr. Citizen Pushpak Pass Rs 830/-
 Vajra Pass Rs 1350/-

 Note: The rates of passes are subjected to change in future. If citizen who holds black board pass he can go anywhere within the city. Pushpak pass have no limitations, except Volvo buses. Vajra Pass Holders can travel in any of the Bus services provided by BMTC

 Visit [https://www.mybmtc.com/] www.mybmtc.com for more details.
