 The Bangalore Development Authority (BDA) came into force with effect from 16th January 1976 
under a separate Act of State Legislature Viz. The BDA Act 1976. The Authority has been established 
with the twin goals of planning functions of the city planning authority and the developmental functions 
as in the erstwhile City Improvement Trust Board (CITB). Bengaluru is a metropolitan city covering an 
area of 1306 Sq. Km attracting people around the world. The Authority aims at checking the haphazard 
and irregular growth in the city. The Garden city has a glorious past with varies cultures, lifestyles and 
enjoys a very salubrious climate.
NEW SCHEMES
Dr.  K. Shivaram Karanth Layout
Dr. K. Shivaram Karanth Layout is one of BDA’s most ambitious residential development initiatives, 
spanning over 3,456 acres. The project is designed to meet the rising demand for affordable housing and 
systematic urban growth in North Bengaluru. With plans for integrated civic amenities, wide road 
networks, green spaces, and essential infrastructure, the layout is being developed in a phased manner. 
During the financial year, substantial budgetary provisions are earmarked for infrastructure execution 
and utility service provisioning to expedite allotment.
Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Layout
Nadaprabhu Kempegowda Layout, a flagship BDA project, aims to provide quality residential 
infrastructure across 4,043 acres in West Bengaluru. Layout is planned with state-of-the-art 
infrastructure including water supply, underground drainage, electricity, and wide roads. This year’s 
budget focuses on accelerating civil works, improving public utility services, and expediting site 
allotments. Provision has also been made for green zone development, stormwater drains and timely 
project completion under the revised master plan.
Hebbala Unidirectional Underpass over and widening of Flyover
The Hebbala Unidirectional Underpass project over the Outer Ring Road and widening of Flyover is a 
critical traffic decongestion initiative by BDA to ease vehicular movement in North Bengaluru. Designed 
to streamline traffic flow towards Kempegowda International Airport and beyond, the underpass will 
significantly reduce travel time and enhance road safety. The current year’s budget includes provisions 
for civil works, traffic diversion planning, stormwater drainage integration and advanced lighting 
systems. 
Lakes Under BDA
BDA continues to prioritize the conservation and rejuvenation of Bengaluru’s lakes to enhance ecological 
balance and urban biodiversity. Lakes under BDA’s jurisdiction are being developed with fencing, 
desilting, walking tracks, STPs, and wetland zones to support flora and fauna. This year, the budget 
provides for lake bund strengthening, inlet/outlet works and maintenance contracts. 
Bangalore Business Corridor
The Bangalore Business Corridor (BBC) is a visionary infrastructure project takes off on Tumkur road 
and terminates at Hosur road totaling 73.04 Kms. aimed to alleviate traffic congestion and improve 
connectivity in the region catalyzing commercial and economic growth across the city’s key growth zones. 
Strategically connecting multiple industrial, IT, and logistics hubs, the corridor includes provision for 
eight-lane roadways, integrated townships, and high-capacity transport systems. In this budget cycle, 
allocations are made for land acquisition, DPR preparation, and early-stage road works.

Capital Expenditure
(1) BDA has been instrumental in developing various layouts with a view to provide better quality of life by 
ensuring sustainable and planned development.  A provision of Rs.625.26 Crs have been kept for the 
left out infrastructure works at Arkavathi layout, BSK 6th Stage, J.P. Nagar 8th Phase and at 
Anjanapura.
(2) Nadaprabhu Kempegowda layout is one of the largest layouts formed by BDA in recent years. 22,521 
sites are allotted to general public, farmers, etc,. A provision of Rs.1543.28 Crs is made to complete 
the ongoing infrastructure works.
(3) BDA with its objective of developing high quality layouts has undertaken the development and allot 
sites at Dr.K.Shivaram Karanth Layout. The work is undertaking at a brisk pace.  A Provision of 
Rs.1300.00 is made for final works like road formation, electricity works, Water supply etc.,
(4) To meet the ever increasing demand for residential accommodation, BDA has undertaken the 
construction of flats at many locations in Bangalore Rs.559.41  Crs is allocated for the construction of 
flats.
(5) BDA is in the process of preparation of RMP-2041.  Hence Rs.18.99 Crs is allocated in the current 
year to meet expenses w.r.t RMP-2041.
(6) Rs.29.00 Crs is allocated to meet various activities like purchase of vehicle / furniture / computers / 
softwares/ computerisation activities.

Revenue Expenditure
  1. Rs.14.30 Crs is allocated for maintenance of BDA Office buildings and shopping complexes 
and Rs.29.17 Crs is allocated for maintenance works at  old BDA Layouts handed over to 
BBMP.
  2. BDA has never lost sight of its objective to develop more green areas with a view to provide 
healthy and better environment to the citizens of Bangalore.  It is proposed to enchance the 
“Green Cover” @ SMV Layout, NPKL, BSK & Arkavathi Layouts. Rs.15.00 Crs is earmarked 
for these activities.
  3. BDA has been instrumental in developing various flyovers, underpasses, ROB’s and such 
other infrastructure works.  An amount of Rs.170.00 Crs has been provided towards 
completing the on-going and New infrastructure works.
  4. BDA has taken up reconstruction of Malleshwaram Market, which is nearing completion. 
Rs.33.79 Crs is earmarked for the reconstruction of markets
  5. Lakes are the lung spaces of a city and climate moderators adding to thermal ambience.  Lakes 
have a direct bearing on the quality of life in urban areas.  Lakes play a great role in urbanised 
areas to capture rainwater efficiently to facilitate ground water re-charge.  BDA has 5 lakes 
under its control viz Bellanduru lake , Varthuru Lake, Chikkabanawara Lake, Ramasandra 
Lake and Kommaghatta Lake.
  6. Following budget provision is made for rejuvenation and development of lakes
   (1) Varthur Lake Rs.34.00Cr
   (2) Other Lakes Rs.1.00 Cr
  7. Trees are important for our survival as well as the ecosystem. Without trees, life would not have 
been possible.  To make greener layouts, BDA is embarking on “Plantation in BDA developed 
layouts”.  Rs.13.63 Crs is earmarked for this programme. BDA is also undertaking 
afforestation works at BDA Layouts out of grants received from K-Ride as per the directions of 
NGT and Karnataka High Court.
Payment of Interest
BDA has cleared the loans obtained and not intending to obtain any loans.  Hence no provision is made for 
servicing of interest.
