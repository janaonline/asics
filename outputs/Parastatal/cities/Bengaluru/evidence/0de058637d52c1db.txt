Shakti Scheme  
As per the direction of the  Government of Karnataka (Government Order No. TD 18 TCB 
2023 dated 05.06.2023), the BMTC has made arrangeme nts for women passengers 
(including girl students) and transgenders in the s tate of Karnataka to travel free of cost 
in the ordinary services (excluding air-conditioned vehicles) of the corporation from 
11.06.2023 to empower them economically and enable them to participate in all 
spheres of life.              
Free travel facility under Shakti Scheme:  
• Women passengers (including girl students) and transgender of Karnataka State 
are allowed to travel free of cost in the ordinary services of the Corporation 
(including Non-AC electric vehicles). 
• Following Identity cards with photo and residential  address issued by Government 
of India/Government of Karnataka/ Government Owned Offices are considered 
(having the details of residence) to avail the free  travel facility under Shakti 
Scheme : 
 Passport issued by Government of India, 
 Voter ID issued by Election Commission, 
 Aadhaar Card, 
 Driving License, 
 National Rural Employment Guarantee Act Identity Ca rd (NREGA card) with 
photograph & residential address, 
 Identity card with photograph & residential address  issued by Government of 
India/Government of Karnataka Department/Public Sector Units (PSU's), 
 Identity card issued by Directorate of Disabled and  Senior Citizens Welfare, 
Government of Karnataka. 
 Transgender ID  
• ID cards can be produce in original/ Xerox/ Digilocker (hard / soft copy) formats. 
• Girl students studying school/college can produce identity card.
