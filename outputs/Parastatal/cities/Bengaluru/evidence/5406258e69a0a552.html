BWSSB Billing Services – Version 2.0

Bangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB) has developed web-based consumer-friendly applications for revenue billing, collection, online payment and citizen services.

Major Services

1. SAJALA – Centralized Billing and Collection System which forms an important IT system component of BWSSB. It maintains consumer billing and revenue collection information. Billing activity is carried out through hand-held spot billing devices with GPRS technology.

2. Payment Portal – BWSSB provides citizens with online payment options for water bills.

3. Consumer-oriented Services – Citizen services include:

  • Replacement of faulty meters
  • Testing of faulty meters
  • Transfer of ownership
  • Issuing of Certificate for Non-Availability of Water from the water supply agency required for NOC for water abstraction from the ground water authority
  • Permission for drilling new borewells within BBMP limits
