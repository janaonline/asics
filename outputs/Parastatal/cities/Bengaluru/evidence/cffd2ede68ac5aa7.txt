KARNATAKA LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY 
SIXTEENTH LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY 
EIGHTH SESSION 
 
THE BENGALURU METROPOLITAN LAND TRANSPORT AUTHORITY 
(AMENDMENT) BILL, 2025 
(LA Bill No. 83 of 2025) 
 
A Bill to amend the Bengaluru Metropolitan Land Transport Authority  
Act, 2022. 
Whereas it is expedient to amend the Bengaluru Metropolitan Land Transport 
Authority Act, 2022 (Karnataka Act 06 of 2023) for the purposes hereinafter 
appearing: 
Be it enacted by the Karnataka State Legislature in the seventy sixth year of 
the Republic of India as follows, namely:- 
1. Short title and commencement .- (1) This Act may be called the 
Bengaluru Metropolitan Land Transport Authority (Amendment) Act, 2025. 
(2) It shall come into force at once. 
2. Amendment of section 2.- In the Bengaluru Metropolitan Land 
Transport Authority Act, 2022 (Karnataka Act 06 of 2023) (herein after referred to 
as the Principal Act), in section 2, in clause (cc), for the words, brackets and figures 
“Bruhath Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike as defined in the Bruhat Bengaluru 
Mahanagara Palike Act, 2020” (Karnataka Act 53 of 2020)”,  the words, brackets 
and figures “Greater Bengaluru Authority, as defined in the Greater Bengaluru 
Authority, 2024 (Karnataka Act 36 of 2025)” shall be substituted. 
3. Amendment of section 4.-  In the Principal Act, in section 4, in sub-
section (1), - 
(i)  for clause (j), the following shall be substituted, namely:- 
“(j-1) The Mayor of Bengaluru West City Corporation; 
(j-2) The Mayor of Bengaluru South City Corporation; 
(j-3) The Mayor of Bengaluru North City Corporation; 
(j-4) The Mayor of Bengaluru East City Corporation; 
(j-5) The Mayor of Bengaluru Central City Corporation;” 
(ii) for clause (n), the following shall be substituted, namely:- 
“(n-1) The Commissioner of Bengaluru West City Corporation; 
(n-2) The Commissioner of Bengaluru South City Corporation; 
(n-3) The Commissioner of Bengaluru North City Corporation; 
(n-4) The Commissioner of Bengaluru East City Corporation; 
(n-5) The Commissioner of Bengaluru Central City Corporation;” 
 
4.  Amendment of section 12.-  In the Principal Act, in section 12, in sub-
section (1), for clause (c), the following shall be substituted, namely:- 
“(c-1) The Chief Commissioner, Greater Bengaluru Authority;  
(c-2) The Commissioner of Bengaluru West City Corporation;  
(c-3) The Commissioner of Bengaluru South City Corporation;  
(c-4) The Commissioner of Bengaluru North City Corporation;  
(c-5) The Commissioner of Bengaluru East City Corporation; 
(c-6) The Commissioner of Bengaluru Central City Corporation;”

STATEMENT OF OBJECTS AND REASONS 
 
It is considered necessary to amend the Bengaluru Metropolitan Land 
Transport Authority Act, 2022 (Karnataka Act 06 of 2023) to revise the provisions of 
Constitution of Bengaluru Metropolitan Land Transport Authority and Executive 
Committee regarding Ex-officio Members,- 
(1) the Mayor, Bruhat Bengaluru Mahanagara Palike; and  
(2) Chief Commissioner of BBMP. 
 and to insert the City Corporations of Greater Bengaluru Area consequent 
on enactment of Greater Bengaluru Governance Act, 2024. 
Hence, the Bill.
