10. Penalty 
The existing penalty for not providing a Rainwat er Harvesting system, non -submission of the Occupation 
Certificate, and not providing an STP remains the same for both domestic and non -domestic connections. 
However, for connections other than domestic and non-domestic, the charges are applicable at par with non-
domestic connections. 
 
The revised water and sanitation tariff will come into effect from 01-04-2025 in respect of all the consumers as 
per the rates shown above. The billing will be with effect from 01-05-2025. 
 
As per the Karnataka Administrative Reforms Commission's recommendation, a 3% automatic annual increase 
in water tariffs every year 1st of April.
