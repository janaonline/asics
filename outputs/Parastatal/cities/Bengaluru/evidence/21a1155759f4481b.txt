## Online RTI Information System

 Please do not file RTI application/first appeal through this portal for Departments/Public Authorities under Central Government or other State Governments. If filed, the application would be returned without refund of the fee amount.

 This is a portal to file RTI application/first appeal online along with payment gateway. Fee payment can be made through Internet Banking. Through this portal RTI application/first appeal can be filed by Indian Citizen only for the Departments/Public Authorities of the Govt. of Karnataka 

 Please read instructions carefully while submitting the RTI application/first appeal.

 DISCLAIMER

 Centre for e-Governance of DPAR, e-Governance Department is the nodal agency for
managing the RTI Online application portal. The respective departments are responsible for
disposal of RTI applications received online through the portal.

 With reference to point 1, only the issues regarding RTI Online portal, may be addressed to
the RTI Online PMU of CeG. Any complaints with regard to RTI applications, delays in
disposal in stipulated time needs to be submitted to the departmental nodal officers of
respective departments only. The department wise Nodal Officers list is available in the RTI
Online portal.

 The RTI ONLINE Application/Appeal does not have the provision to transfer an application
online, under Section 6 (3) of the RTI Act to any Public Authority where-in RTI ONLINE
facility is still not on-boarded in the portal.

 The RTI online application/appeal can be filed only for the public authorities which are listed
in the public authority menu of the RTI online portal. If RTI application/appeal needs to be
submitted to the public authority which are not listed in this portal, it is recommended to file
RTI Application in Physical Form (Hard Copy) to such Public Authorities.

 Do not Upload UID/Aadhaar Card/Pan Card/Passport/Personal Data.
