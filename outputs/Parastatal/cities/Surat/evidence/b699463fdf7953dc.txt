KEY EVENTS HELD DURING THE YEAR 
1. Inauguration of Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase-1 by Hon’ble Prime Minister 
 
Shri Narendra Modi Hon’ble Prime Minister of India inaugurated the Ahmedabad Metro 
Rail Project Phase-I on 30th September, 2022. The first train of Phase-I was flagged from 
Kalupur Underground Station. Hon’ble Prime Minister also enjoyed the ride of 
Ahmedabad Metro with children and other dignitaries on the day of inauguration. The 
entire network of 38.60 Kms catering metro services on East-West Corridor was opened 
for the Citizens of Ahmedabad from the day of ‘Mahatma Gandhi Jayanti’ on 2nd October, 
2022 and services on North-South Corridor commenced from 6th October, 2022 onwards.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3

[page 7]
2. Visit of Commissioner of Metro Railway Safety (CMRS)  
The inspection process of Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase-1 was 
commenced by CMRS in August, 2022 for East West section and inspection of 
North South section was done in September, 2022. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
GMRC (Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation) diligently addressed and complied with 
the observations raised by CMRS. Accordingly, GMRC was authorized to open 
both sections for general public post completion of inspection process. 
 
3. Special Train Trips during IPL-2023 Matches 
 
The Indian Premier League (IPL)-2023 matches were played at Narendra Modi 
Stadium, Motera, Ahmedabad from 31st March, 2023. In view of this, special trains 
trips were planned with special fare for convenience of the audience. The last train 
trips were operated at 01.00 AM post completion of matches. Due to this facility, 
majority of audience preferred to travel to stadium by Ahmedabad Metro. Around 
75,190 passengers travelled by Ahmedabad Metro on 31 st March, 2023. Further, 
GMRC carried out the train wrapping works for the ‘Gujarat Titans’ team during 
IPL match, which contributed to the Non-Fare Box revenue as well.

[page 8]
4. Supervision and Appraisal Mission of AFD & KfW  
Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase-II and Surat Metro Rail Project Phase-I are 
co-funded by Agence Francaise De Developpement (AFD), France and 
Kreditanstalt für Wiederaufbau (KfW), Germany. 
 
The supervision mission of AFD and KfW was held in February, 2023 with an 
objective to assess the physical progress of the works through site visits, review 
of implementation schedule, procurement plan, status of detailed design studies, 
list of tenders under preparation and awaited NoCs, discussion on Metro+ 
components, financial aspects and operational aspects.   
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Further, the mission also delved on the issues of Environmental & Social (E&S) 
aspects such as updated E&S Safeguard documentation, measures undertaken 
by GMRC to minimize social impacts, climate change mitigation, draft E & S 
Commitment Plan, Monitoring and Evaluation framework of the project including 
legal aspects.

5. Stakeholder Consultation and Public Meetings at Surat 
During the year under review, with an aim to create awareness about the project 
and understand its impact on various stakeholders of the society, various public 
consultation meetings were conducted for Surat Metro Rail Project with Project 
Affected Parties, Non-Affected People, Government Departments, local NGOs, 
etc. 
                 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
6. Hindi Diwas Celebrations 
 
Every year ‘Hindi Diwas’ is celebrated on 14th of September. It marks the day on 
which Hindi language was declared as the official language of Union of India. 
Accordingly, Hindi Diwas was celebrated at GMRCL during the year on 14th 
September, 2022 through online mode for all the employees of Ahmedabad 
Phase-I & II and Surat Project. Various activities like, Speech, Poetry recitation, 
Word games/phrase completion, quiz, etc were held as a part of promotion of 
national language within the organization. The event witnessed good number of 
participation from employees of various departments. In order to encourage 
participations, the winners were awarded with prize.

1. East-West Corridor (Thaltej Gam to Vastral Gam) 
 
1.1 Eastern Reach (Elevated) – Vastral Gam to Apparel Park 
The construction of the project started with the contract for building the Viaduct from 
Vastral Gam to Apparel Park (priority reach 1) being awarded in February 2015. 
The commercial operations on the priority reach section began for 2 stations from 
March 9th, 2019, with an entry/exit point on both sides of the station. Subsequently, 
the construction of the remaining 4 stations was finished by December 2020. 
Following this, permission was granted to operate the metro at an increased speed. 
Additionally, 19 more entry and exit points were progressively commissioned in the 
Eastern Reach of metro system. 
This progress indicates a significant step towards improving public transportation 
and connectivity in Ahmedabad city. 
The route encompasses a stretch that passes through a total of six stations: Vastral 
Gam, Nirant Cross Roads, Vastral, Rabari Colony, Amraiwadi, and Apparel Park 
Stations. Notably, operational activities have been successfully initiated at each of 
these stations. 
All the above six stations were operational during the year under review.

1.2 Apparel Park Depot 
 
The Apparel Park Depot stands as a fully functional establishment catering to the 
stabling requirements of the metro trains traversing the East-West Corridor. This 
facility boasts a comprehensive workshop, serving as a central nucleus for the 
Operation and Maintenance of the metro trains on the network. It houses a covered 
stabling shed that is designed to accommodate 18 train sets, each comprising six 
cars. This depot serves as an integral hub, playing a pivotal role in ensuring 
seamless operations and upkeep of the metro train fleet. 
 
1.3 Eastern Reach (Underground) – Tunnel from Apparel Park to Shahpur 
 
The intricate task of tunneling beneath the historic heart of old city of Ahmedabad, 
spanning a distance of 6.5 kilometers, has been successfully accomplished. This 
stretch required utmost precision and care, owing to presence of numerous heritage 
monuments that adorn this cherished part of the city. The meticulous execution of 
tunneling process in this stretch reflects a deep appreciation for historical 
significance and underscores our commitment to preserve rich cultural heritage of 
the city.

a. Underground Package 1 (Kankaria to Kalupur) 
 
The Kalupur Metro Station has been made fully operational during the year. This 
station located underneath the Ahmedabad Railway Stations acts as a major 
connecting node for the inter-state and intra-state travellers. Further, due the central 
location of this station in old Ahmedabad city area, it provides great relief to the 
commuters in terms of time saving and vehicular pollution due to traffic congestion. 
Your Company is taking diligent efforts to complete the station works at Kankaria 
East by December, 2023. 
 
The integration of Kalupur Station with both the Kalupur Railway Station and the 
National High-Speed Rail Project stands as a testament to a comprehensive Multi 
Modal Integration Plan. This strategic approach not only enhances the efficiency 
and accessibility of the transportation network but also underscores a commitment 
to holistic urban connectivity and mobility. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
b. Underground Package 2: Gheekanta to Shahpur 
The Gheekanta and Shahpur underground metro stations have been successfully
