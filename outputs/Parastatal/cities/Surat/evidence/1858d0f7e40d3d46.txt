Recruitment notification for the post of  JOINT MANAGING DIRECTOR, GMRCL 
1. COMPANY PROFILE: 
 
Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Limited (hereinafter referred as ‘the Company’) is a 50:50 jointly 
owned Special Purpose Vehicle of Govt. of India (GoI) and Govt. of Gujarat (GoG) and is entrusted with the 
responsibility of establishment and operation & maintenance of Rail Based Mass Rapid Transport Services 
(MRTS) within the State of Gujarat. Incorporated in the year February, 2010 as State Government entity, 
GMRCL was converted to 50:50 Joint Venture of GOI & GoG in March, 2015 and rechristened with the 
present name in December, 2018. The Company has an objective to provide  state  of  the  art,  dedicated,  
safe  &  secure,  reliable,  punctual,  cost effective and environment friendly commuting experience to 
the people of the State. 
 
As on date GMRCL has commissioned and operating on total 61 Kms route length with 38 stations of Phase-
I & Phase-II of Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project across two corridors within Ahmedabad City, which further 
extends upto Gandhinagar (i.e. from Motera to Sector-1) in Phase-II and construction works is ongoing for 
remaining 9 kms of stretch with stations in Gandhinagar. The construction activities of 40 kms route length 
including 6.5 kms underground stretch of Surat Metro Rail Project is under progress. Additionally, planning 
for expansion in Ahmedabad & Gandhinagar and starting metros in other cities in Gujarat is also ongoing. 
 
Ahmedabad Metro has emerged as a preferred mode of urban transport over other modes due to its 
affordability, connectivity, comfort and frequency across length and breadth of Ahmedabad City that 
connects various important public nodes. The strategically placed stations augments world class public 
transport experience of the commuters from all walks of life. The Phase-II of the project extending to 
State Capital seamlessly connects the twin-cities of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar. 
 
On an average over one lakh people commute through Ahmedabad Metro every day and these numbers 
are bound to increase substantially in the coming time with the increase in route length and addition 
of stations.

2.  JOB DESCRIPTION AND RESPONSIBILITIES:  
The Joint Managing Director will assist MD in the overall functioning of the organization. The incumbent 
will be responsible for successful implementation of Projects assigned to GMRCL within the approved 
budgets and timelines. As the leader of approx. 900 work force of Gujarat Metro, the individual will be 
responsible for smooth operations of the Metro system. He/She will also be responsible for planning of 
expansion of GMRCL Project and should initiate timely action including preparation of DPR, tying up of 
funds and arranging Govt. approvals. He/She should be able to run the metro operations with a vision 
of affordability of the services to the common man yet ensuring sustainability of the system on its 
own.  
He/She will be overall responsible for all construction activities, planning, scheduling, monitoring 
and controlling of preliminary and final designs, tendering, civil works, track works, contract

administration and management, monitoring of construction and commissioning etc. He/She will 
be responsible for project monitoring, interface management, quality assurance and safety, cost 
control of works under his charge, budgeting activities, land acquisition, rehabilitation of project 
affected persons/ business, utility and traffic diversions etc. required for construction works. 
He/She shall be responsible for planning extension of Project Lines.

II. QUALIFICATION AND EXPERIENCE: 
 
The candidate must have a degree in Engineering (Civil) from a University or Institute recognized 
by the Government of India or a State Government.  
 
ESSENTIAL EXPERIENCE OF PROSPECTIVE CANDIDATE: 
 
A. A proven track record of minimum Thirty (30) years of Group A / Executive Service in Railways or 
equivalent, including Railway PSUs/ Metro Rail Corporation. 
 
B. Should have a Minimum Twenty (20) years of experience out of Thirty (30) years mentioned above in 
Planning and Construction of Multidisciplinary Railway Projects, including experience in land acquisition 
and rehabilitation and resettlement of project affected persons. 
 
C. Should have extensive experience in preparing tenders / contract management and project 
management of important engineering structures such as Bridges/Tunnels/ Underground Construction. 
 
D. Should have experience and knowledge of coordinating and interfacing with Railway related disciplines 
such as Track laying works, signaling electrification and rolling stock etc. 
 
E. Should be well versed with safety and quality aspects relating to Metro Rail/ Railway Construction and 
Operations. 
 
F. Should have substantial experience of coordination with State Government. 
 
In addition of the above, experience in elevated construction such as Viaduct and all aspects of large 
projects on international contracts i.e. planning, Bid Documents. Bidding, Execution, Commissioning, 
etc. and having knowledge of complying with the project related requirements of international funding 
institutions will be preferable.  
Applicants who have worked for at least 1(one) year in the pay scale of Rs.150000 – 300000 (IDA) or 
equivalent pay scale in CDA or equivalent in Government / PSU will have an added advantage for this 
position.
