The Surat Municipal Corporation (SMC) has responded to the challengers  of  fastest  
population growth and high speed economic development by adopting the best urban 
management practices. The administration of SMC with the help of the people and 
elected members of the  city has transformed Surat to one of the cleanest cities of India. 
SMC has  taken all necessary steps to make the city a better place to live all  amenities.  
SMC has taken  up many path breaking initiatives and these efforts have been 
acknowledged at national and international level. 
1.2 About Surat Sitilink Ltd.(SSL) 
 
Surat Municipal Corporation has formed a separate Special Purpose Vehicle(SPV) as 
Surat Sitilink Ltd.(SSL)  for the implementation of projects for public transportation for 
the city of Surat and thier citizens. This SPV shall carry end to end responsibility for bus 
operations.

3. Terms  of  References for Internal Audit of Surat Sitilink Ltd. 
The proposed scope of Internal Audit to be carried out for the 0  1.04.2026 to 31.03.2027 
while the reporting will be based on 3-month periods June, September, December and 
March. It is a comprehensive scope covering verification of all the projects carried out by 
the Surat Sitilink Ltd- Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) of Surat Municipal Corporation. It aims 
to give broad contours within which the audit would be carried out; however, it may 
evolve in the other areas based on the necessity, mandate and compulsion. The Scope is 
divided into following broad segments: 
3.1   System & Procedural Audit 
3.1.1   A  ccounts and Finance 
 
a) Fund Management including Drawing Segregation route between “A&OE”, “Funds 
received from Surat Municipal Corporation” and other funds out of Fixed deposits.  
b) Grant received from government. 
c) System and process for JV’s and controls related thereto.  
d) Compliance with various Accounting Standards.  
e) MIS - To ensure that the Information System is seamlessly integrated and has 
minimal manual intervention and there are adequate controls on financial and 
operational reporting. 
 
 3.1.2   S  tatutory compliances 
 
 
a) Verification of Investments & Investment Register.  
b) Contingent Liabilities 
c) Verification of Secretarial Compliances - To check all statutory records   
registers including Minute Books and to see that accounting effects of all 
the decisions taken at Board / Committee Meeting / General Meeting /  
Audit Committee in the Books of Accounts. 
d) Review on the Company’s dealing where related parties are interested.  
e) Fixed Assets Verification with Fixed Assets Register.  
 
 
 

[page 8]

3.2  Pre-audit of transactions 
 
      3.2.1 Transaction & Compliance Audit 
 
a) Pre-audit of transactions before they are vouched for in the accounts.  
b) Contractual Arrangements verification with individuals and other entities.  
c) Stamp & Signature on each of the transactions as a token of pre-audit for 
processing the documents. 
d) Verification of Cash and Bank payments, FDR’s, Security Deposits and receipt 
system. 
e) Review of all ledgers, Balance sheet and Profit & loss Account on quarterly basis.  
f) Visit as frequently as necessary so  that no transaction remains unprocessed  as a 
result of pre-audit for more than 24 working hrs.  
g) Opinion related to Direct Tax and Indirect Tax. 
h) Penalty maybe levied on the Internal Auditor for the delay, non-compliance, or any 
work related dissatisfaction at office of SSL. The amount of penalty will be 
determined by the management. Additionally, SSL reserves the right to deduct 
from the final fee payment at the discretion of management, if the work is not 
perform as per the work order. 
i) During the execution of the contract, if any change is made in the rate of GST 
(Goods and Services Tax) by the government, it will be refunded/collected 
separately by Surat Sitilink Ltd. For which the successful contractor will have to 
inform the competent authority along with the original receipt/proof of GST paid to 
the government within 30 days from the date of payment of the bill along with the 
certificate of their Chartered Accountant. 
 
 
3.2.2  P  ayroll and HR Related Documents 
 
a) Verification of salaries and statutory deductions related documents e.g. Provident      
    Fund, ESI, Professional Tax etc.

3.2.3  B  udget: 
  
a) Base of the budget to be verified to ensure that sanctity of the budget is

b) All the transactions entered into form part of the approved projects and

3.2.4 O  ther Areas / Responsibilities: 
 
a) Providing consultancy on various matters related to Compliances.
