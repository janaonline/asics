OUTCOME BUDGET 2026-27 
The Outcome Budget 2026-27 is the tenth of its kind since the Government of Gujarat initiated an 
Outcome Budget in the year 2017 -18. The Outcome Budget also aims at changing the perspective by 
shifting the focus from ‘outlays’ to measurable ‘outcomes’ and can be viewed as a tangible expression of the 
Government’s priorities, performances, decisions and intentions. The budget publication n o. 56 is attributed 
to Outcome Budget . The Outcome Budget establish es linkage between budgetary provision and socially 
viable development outcome across schemes and programmes.  
In the context of UN 2030 Agenda of Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), the State Government 
has prepared “G ujarat Sustainable Vision 2030” which comprises of State Indicator Framework (SIF). The 
schemes and programmes of Outcome Budget have been linked with State Indicator Framework. The 
progress of various development schemes is  being monitored at highest level of the Government through 
CM Dashboard. 
This publication will serve as a valuable reference within the framework of the Viksit Gujarat @ 2047 
roadmap, contributing to the broader vision of Viksit Bharat @ 2047. It offers meaningful insights supported 
by tangible outcomes outlined for the next three financial years, thereby guiding progress toward s these 
long-term development goals. 
Generally, schemes having large outlays, schemes related to physical output such as constructi on and 
procurement, schemes intended to improve developmental indicators and schemes designed to extend 
subsidies or benefits to in dividuals, have been included. Thereafter indicators have been rationalized by the 
respective Department for each of these sc hemes and physical targets have been i ndicated for the next 3 
years. These targets are projections and are subject to modifications based on actual requirements and

budgetary provisions of the relevant year.  A special attempt has been made to indicate Macr o Outcomes for

For the year 2026-27, Planning Division, General Administration Department has carried out extensive 
consultations with Departments to rationalize targets and priorities. 
Another exercise that has been taken in tandem with the Outcome Budget is the revision of the Annual 
Development Programme to include the developmental objectives and strategies for each Department and 
the sector that they are dealing with. The schematic details such as the components and activities undertaken 
under the scheme, intended beneficiaries, the scale of assistance, if applicable, implementing agency, the 
funding pattern and the intended outcomes have also been articulated for these selected schem es and 
included in Part-II of the Budget Publication no. 35: Development Programme.  
It is expected tha t Outcome Budget would help in decision making to arrive at a better understanding 
of the efficiency and effectiveness of Government spendings. This will also serve as a useful monitoring tool 
to track progress and ensure the effective achievement of the vision outlined in the Viksit Gujarat @ 2047 
roadmap.
