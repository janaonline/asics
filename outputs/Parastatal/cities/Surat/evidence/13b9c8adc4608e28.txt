Project Overview:

Ahmedabad Metro Project, Phase-I having two corridors i.e. North-South Corridor- APMC to Motera Stadium and East-West Corridor, Thaltej Gam to Vastral Gam was sanctioned by Government of India (GoI) in November, 2014.

The commercial operations of the Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase-I began in March 2019 with 6.5 km viaduct and initial opening of Vastral Gam & Apparel Park stations, followed by Nirant Cross Road in April 2019, Amraiwadi in May 2019 and Vastral & Rabari Colony in March’21. Further, 32.1 km stretch became operational in October 2022. An additional 1.43 km stretch from Thaltej to Thaltej Gam opened for public in December 2024 , bringing the total span of Ahmedabad Metro Phase-I to 40.03 km with 31 stations.

Project Overview:

Ahmedabad Metro Phase-II is an extension of Phase-I, aimed at connecting the twin cities of Ahmedabad and Gandhinagar, and comprises two corridors with a total length of 28.2 km and 22 stations. Of this, 20.8 km with 8 stations, covering the Motera to Sector-1 and GNLU to GIFT City sections, was inaugurated by the Hon’ble Prime Minister on 16th September 2024. Further, final section till Mahatma Mandir was inaugurated by the Hon’ble Prime Minister on 11th January 2026, marking the full commissioning of Ahmedabad Metro Phase-II.

Project Overview:

Surat Metro Project comprises of two corridors : Corridor-1 -Sarthana to Dream city and Corridor 2- Bhesan to Saroli having 38 stations with a total length of 40.35 km of which 6.47 km is underground. The two (02) depots are located at Dream City and Bhesan. The civil construction work is under progress.

Project Overview:

Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase 2(A) corridor consisting of 6.032 km length & 05 stations (04-elevated & 01 UG) will provide direct, rapid connectivity of Ahmedabad, Gandhinagar and GIFT to Sardar Vallabhbhai International Airport.  The project will enhance multimodal integration by connecting the airport with major transport hubs, including the High-Speed Rail Station, railway stations, bus terminals, BRTS etc. It will also improve connectivity to residential and industrial areas such as Koteshwar, Bhat, and the Sabarmati Riverfront. Furthermore, it will serve as the main mode of transport for the upcoming Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel Sports Enclave during the World Police Games 2029 and the Commonwealth Games 2030.

Project Overview:

The Ahmedabad Metro Rail Project Phase-2B (GIFT City (excluding) to Shahpur) is an extension of the existing Ahmedabad–Gandhinagar metro network aimed at improving connectivity between Ahmedabad and GIFT City through a 3.33 km elevated corridor consisting of 03 stations. The project will improve access to GIFT City, India’s emerging global financial and technology hub, along with nearby urban areas, educational institutions, residential neighbourhoods, and commercial centres.
