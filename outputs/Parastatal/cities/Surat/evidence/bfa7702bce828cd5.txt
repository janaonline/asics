## Proactive Disclosure under the Provision of Section 4(1)(B) of RTI ACT 2005

CHAPTER 1 : PARTICULARS OF ORGANIZATION/FUNCTIONS AND DUTIES

Gujarat Metro Rail Corporation (GMRC) Limited was incorporated on 4th February, 2010 as a Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) by the Government of Gujarat to implement Metro Rail Project under the Companies Act, 1956. The company was restructured and with effect from 20th March 2015 the Company has been converted as a 50: 50 SPV of Government of India and Government of Gujarat.

CHAPTER 3 : PROCEDURES FOLLOWED IN DECISION MAKING PROCESS INCLUDING CHANNELS OR SUPERVISION AND ACCOUNTABILITY

The decision making process of the Company follows the following channel:

BOARD OF DIRECTORS  -> MANAGING DIRECTOR -> FUNCTIONAL HEADS

Overall Management of the Company is vested with the Board of Directors of the Company. The Board of Directors is the highest decision making body within the Company.

The day-to-day management of the company in entrusted to the Managing Director andother Directors and Functional heads of the Company.

The Managing Director is accountable to Board of Directors for proper discharge of theirduties and responsibilities.

CHAPTER 4 : THE NORMS SET FOR DISCHARGE OF FUNCTIONS

The Company has well defined procedures and guidelines for smooth operations and its officers discharge their respective functions within the norms set through:

Delegation of Powers issued to the Managing Director, Functional Directors and to the officers of the Company at various levels. The exercise of powers should not infringe upon different provisions of the Companies Act, Government Guidelines and any other statutory/regulatory requirement. The delegation of powers is subject to observance of Memorandum of Association, Articles of Association, relevant policy, guidelines and administrative instructions of the Company.

Policies and Guidelines issued by the Company.

Manuals/Instruction: The Company has procedural manuals covering all important activities viz. Personnel Manual, Accounts Manual and Internal Audit Manual.

Guidelines of Department of Public Enterprises.

Guidelines of Chief Vigilance Commission.

CHAPTER 17 :ANY OTHER / GENERAL INFORMATION

The right to information means that the citizens have right to obtain any information(except those exempted from disclaims) under the control of Public Authority i.e.Government and substantially financed by the Government. The information can beobtained:

By inspection of records

By taking Notes and extracts

By taking certified copies

By taking certified sample of material

We can also obtain the information in form of diskettes, floppies, tapes and videocassettes or in any other electronic mode or through printouts.

Who can obtain the information:-Every citizen of India has been provided with the Right to obtain information underthe Right to Information Act, 2005.

Fees to Obtain Information:-

 1 Fee along with the application for requestRs.20
 2 Fee for inspection of files NIL for first half an hour,Rs.20 for every 30 minutesthereafter
3 For A-4 and A-3 size paper created or copied Rs.2 each page
 4For copy in larger size paper Actual charge of cost
5 For samples or models Actual cost or price
6For information provided in diskette or floppy Rs.50 per floppy
7 Fee for people below poverty line NIL
8 Fee in case information is not provided within stipulated time NIL

Time limit under Right to Information Act, 2005:-

 1 Where an application is made to Public Information Officer (PIO) Within 30 days
 2 Where an application is made to Assistant Public Information Officer (APIO) Within 35 days
 3 Where any part of application is transferred to any other public authority Within 35 days
 4Where the information concerns the life of liberty of a person Within 48 days
5  Where the information in respect of allegation of violation of human rights is sought from intelligence and security organization specifiedin the second schedule Within 45 days
 6 Where the Public Information Officers sendsan intimation to the person for furtherpayment of fees of cost of providinginformation Within a period of 30days + period betweendispatch of intimationand payment of furtherfee
 7 Where the information related to third party is 30 days + 5 days forsought 30 days + 5 days forintimation+ periodbetween dispatch andreceipt of notice + 10daysformakingsubmission by third party+ 40 days for decision bythe PIO
