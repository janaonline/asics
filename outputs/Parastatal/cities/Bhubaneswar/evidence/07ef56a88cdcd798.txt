 AMA E-RIDE 

AMA E-Ride, an initiative by Capital Region Urban Transport (CRUT), aims at providing clean, eco-friendly, first and last-mile connectivity to the public through e-rickshaws. With the introduction of AMA E-Ride service, CRUT is committed to providing seamless, reliable and affordable feeder transportation services to the citizens of Bhubaneswar. 

Launched to transform the urban public transport scenario, AMA E-Ride is a significant step towards achieving our goal of providing the best connectivity possible to commuters as CRUT is the first Public Transport Agency in India to manage and have its own fleet of e-rickshaws. AMA E-Rides are equipped with smart technology, ensuring customers a safe and secure ride.
