ବାର୍ତ୍ତା

ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର ଉନ୍ନୟନ କର୍ତ୍ତୃପକ୍ଷ ଆସନ୍ତା ସେପ୍ଟେମ୍ୱର ୧ ତାରିଖରେ ନିଜର ୪୩ତମ ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା ଦିବସ ପାଳନ ଏବଂ ଏହି ଅବସରରେ ବିଭାଗର ବାର୍ଷିକ ବିବରଣୀ-୨୦୨୫ ପ୍ରକାଶ କରୁଥିବା ଜାଣି ମୁଁ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଆନନ୍ଦିତ ।

ବିଡିଏର ଏହି ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା ଦିବସ ପାଳନ ଏକ ଗୌରବମୟ ଯାତ୍ରାର ପ୍ରତୀକ, ଯେଉଁଥିରେ ଆମେ ଓଡ଼ିଶାର ରାଜଧାନୀକୁ ଏକ ଆଧୁନିକ, ସମୃଦ୍ଧ ଓ ସୁସ୍ଥ ମହାନଗରୀରେ ପରିଣତ କରିବା ପାଇଁ ଅହରହ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରୁଛୁ । ଏହି ଅବସରରେ ମୁଁ ଆମର ସମସ୍ତ ଅଧିକାରୀ, କର୍ମଚାରୀ, ଅଂଶୀଦାର ଏବଂ ନାଗରିକମାନଙ୍କୁ ଆନ୍ତରିକ କୃତଜ୍ଞତା ଜଣାଉଛି, ଯେଉଁମାନେ ଏହି ଯାତ୍ରାରେ ଆମର ଅବିଚ୍ଛେଦ୍ୟ ଅଂଶ ହୋଇଛନ୍ତି ।

ଗତ ୪୩ବର୍ଷ ଧରି ବିଡିଏ ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱରକୁ ଏକ ବିଶ୍ୱସ୍ତରୀୟ ସହର ଭାବେ ଗଢ଼ିବାରେ ଅଗ୍ରଣୀ ଭୂମିକା ଗ୍ରହଣ କରିଛି । ସ୍ମାର୍ଟ ସିଟି ଉଦ୍ୟମ, ସବୁଜ ପରିବେଶ ସୁରକ୍ଷା, ଆଧୁନିକ ଭିତ୍ତିଭୂମି ଏବଂ ସୁସ୍ଥ ସହର ପରିକଳ୍ପନା ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ ଏକ ସମୃଦ୍ଧ ଓ ସମାବେଶୀ ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱରର ସ୍ୱପ୍ନକୁ ସାକାର କରିଛୁ । ଆମର ଏହି ପ୍ରୟାସ କେବଳ ଅଟ୍ଟାଳିକା ଓ ରାସ୍ତାଘାଟ ନିର୍ମାଣରେ ସୀମିତ ନୁହେଁ, ବରଂ ଏକ ସୁଖମୟ, ସୁରକ୍ଷିତ ଓ ସମ୍ମାନଜନକ ଜୀବନଶୈଳୀ ସୃଷ୍ଟି କରିବାର ଅଙ୍ଗୀକାର ।

ଏହି ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା ଦିବସରେ ଆମେ ଆମର ପୂର୍ବ ସଫଳତାକୁ ସ୍ମରଣ କରୁଛୁ ଏବଂ ଭବିଷ୍ୟତରେ ଆହୁରି ଉନ୍ନତ ଓ ସ୍ଥାୟୀ ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର ଗଢ଼ିବା ପାଇଁ ସଂକଳ୍ପବଦ୍ଧ ହେଉଛୁ । ଆମର ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ହେଉଛି, ଆମେ ଏମିତି ଏକ ସହର ଗଢ଼ିବା, ଯେଉଁଠାରେ ପ୍ରତ୍ୟେକ ନାଗରିକଙ୍କ ସ୍ୱପ୍ନ ଓ ଆକାଂକ୍ଷା ପୂରଣ ହେବ, ଯେଉଁଠାରେ ଓଡ଼ିଶାର ସଂସ୍କୃତି ଓ ଆଧୁନିକତାର ଏକ ଅପୂର୍ବ ସମନ୍ୱୟ ସୃଷ୍ଟି ହେବ । ଆସନ୍ତୁ, ଆମେ ସମସ୍ତେ ମିଳିତ ଭାବେ ଏହି ଯାତ୍ରାକୁ ଆହୁରି ଗୌରବମୟ କରିବା ପାଇଁ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟ କରିବା ।

ଏହି ଅବସରରେ ମୁଁ ବିଡିଏର ସମସ୍ତ ଅଧିକାରୀ ଓ କର୍ମଚାରୀଙ୍କୁ ହାର୍ଦ୍ଦିକ ଶୁଭେଚ୍ଛା ଜଣାଇବା ସହିତ ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା ଦିବସର ସମସ୍ତ କାର୍ଯ୍ୟକ୍ରମ ଓ ବାର୍ଷିକ ବିବରଣୀ ପ୍ରକାଶନର ସମସ୍ତ ସଫଳତା କାମନା କରୁଛି ।

(ଡ଼ା. କୃଷ୍ଣ ଚନ୍ଦ୍ର ମହାପାତ୍ର)

ବାର୍ତ୍ତା

"ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର ଉନ୍ନୟନ କର୍ତ୍ତୃପକ୍ଷ (ବିଡିଏ)"ର ୪୩ତମ ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା ଉତ୍ସବ ଆଗାମୀ ସେପ୍ଟେମ୍ୱର ୧ ତାରିଖରେ ପାଳନ କରାଯାଉଥିବା ସହ ଏହି ଅବସରରେ ଏହାର ବାର୍ଷିକ ବିବରଣୀ-୨୦୨୫ ପ୍ରକାଶ ପାଉଥିବା ଜାଣି ମୁଁ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଆନନ୍ଦିତ ।

ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର ସହରବାସୀଙ୍କ ନିମନ୍ତେ ଗୁଣାତ୍ମକ ଭିତ୍ତିଭୂମି ଏବଂ ଆଧୁନିକ ସୁବିଧା ସୁଯୋଗ ଦେବାରେ 'ବିଡିଏ'ର ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଭୂମିକା ରହିଛି । କ୍ରମବର୍ଦ୍ଧିଷ୍ଣୁ ଜନସଂଖ୍ୟା ସହିତ ଭୁବନେଶ୍ୱର ସହରର କଳେବର ବୃଦ୍ଧି ପାଉଥିବା ବେଳେ ଏକ ଦ୍ରୁତ ଅଭିବୃଦ୍ଧିଶୀଳ ସହର ଦୃଷ୍ଟିକୋଣରୁ ଏହା ଶିକ୍ଷାବିତ, ବ୍ୟବସାୟୀ, ସୂଚନା-ପ୍ରଯୁକ୍ତିବିଦ୍ୟା ବୃତ୍ତିଧାରୀ ଏବଂ ଶିଳ୍ପପତିମାନଙ୍କ ପାଇଁ ପ୍ରମୁଖ ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟସ୍ଥଳ ଓ ପସନ୍ଦର ସ୍ଥାନ ହୋଇଛି । ନୂତନ ପ୍ରକଳ୍ପ ପରିକଳ୍ପନାଠାରୁ ଆରମ୍ଭ କରି ଏହାର ସଠିକ ରୂପାୟନରେ 'ବିଡିଏ'ର ସାମୂହିକ ପ୍ରୟାସ ଅତ୍ୟନ୍ତ ଗୁରୁତ୍ୱପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ । ବିଶେଷଜ୍ଞିକ ଜ୍ଞାନ ଅଭିଜ୍ଞତା ସମ୍ପନ୍ନ ମାନବ ସମ୍ବଳର ଦକ୍ଷପୂର୍ଣ୍ଣ ଉପଯୋଗ ମାଧ୍ୟମରେ 'ବିଡିଏ' ଏହାର ଲକ୍ଷ୍ୟ ସାଧନରେ ଅଧିକ ସଫଳତା ଅର୍ଜନ କରିବ ବୋଲି ମୋର ଦୃଢ଼ ଆଶା ଓ ବିଶ୍ୱାସ ।

ବିଡିଏର ୪୩ତମ ପ୍ରତିଷ୍ଠା ଦିବସ ଉପଲକ୍ଷେ ମୁଁ ଏହାର ସମସ୍ତ କର୍ତ୍ତୃପକ୍ଷ, ଅଧିକାରୀ, କର୍ମଚାରୀ ତଥା ଶୁଭେଚ୍ଛୁମାନଙ୍କୁ ଆନ୍ତରିକ ଅଭିନନ୍ଦନ ଜଣାଇବା ସହ ଉତ୍ସବର ସଫଳତା କାମନା କରୁଛି ।

(ମନୋଜ ଆହୁଜା)

PLANNING BRANCH

(Activities & Achievements during year 2024 -2025)

1. Preparation of Town Planning Scheme:

Preparation and implementation of Town Planning Schemes in the Bhubaneswar has been taken up under the provisions contained in Chapter VI of the ODA Act, 1982 and Rules framed there under in Chapter VI of the ODA Rules, 1983. Town Planning Scheme branch has been constituted in BDA under Planning Section.

As per the Comprehensive Development Plan-2010, there is a provision of developing a Ring Road of 64.90 km within Bhubaneswar Development Plan Area. Bhubaneswar Development Authority has proposed to take up 45 Town Planning Schemes in 58 Revenue part Villages along the proposed Ring Road as per the CDP to BDPA, so that it will be helpful in getting the land for ring road development without going for land acquisition. All these 45 TP Schemes will cover a vast area of 9171.81 acres (approx.) of land.

GUIDING PRINCIPLES FOR DESIGN OF CHILD FRIENDLY PUBLIC SPACES
The design of each of applicable component shall be prepared using following guiding principles.

NON-NEGOTIABLE PRINCIPLES

Accessibility
Accessibility to a space defines the users' ability to use the said space with ease, without putting in a lot of effort. A Child should have a provision of hindrance free access to safe and vibrant play spaces.

Safety
A child should be and feel safe at all times. Public spaces should be designed in a way that children feel themselves to be a part of their surroundings and occupy 'safe spaces' that enable them to participate in activities with some independence.

Legibility
A legible design approach provides seamless navigational informational alternatives to the user, allowing anyone to access or utilize a space effortlessly, irrespective of their socio-cultural backgrounds, age & abilities. It also instills sense of safety & comfort through unobstructed visibility.

VALUE ADDITION

Environment
Besides, providing a welcoming, clean, safe, accessible & legible spaces, a city must offer a wide array of programs to create & foster child-friendly environments. It is necessary for the child to feel safe and acquainted to his surroundings.

Learn
A child's outdoor experience should nurture beautiful learning experiences for a child, and they should be able to engage in active knowledge sharing with their surroundings.

11. Works related to GIS:

The GIS (Geographic Information System) Branch is an important support wing of BDA, providing mapping and spatial data services for different activities of the Authority. It prepares and updates maps for ongoing projects and assists other branches in their day-to-day work.

A key responsibility of the GIS Branch is the verification of plots in connection with building plan approvals, layout approvals, and other statutory processes. This ensures accuracy in land-related matters and supports smooth approval procedures.

The Branch also facilitates the issue of lease NOCs for alienation of Government land by various Government Departments. It further provides views on conversion of kisam of land from agricultural to non-agricultural purposes, which is essential for planned urban development.

Apart from these core functions, the GIS Branch also supports in the preparation of the Comprehensive Development Plan (CDP) and provides assistance in RTI replies, legal matters, and other official requirements where spatial information is necessary.

Through these activities, the GIS Branch plays a vital role in strengthening the planning, regulatory, and developmental functions of BDA.

Activities & Achievements During Year 2024-25

The Enforcement Wing of BDA is now functioning with Joint Commissioner (Enforcement) as the Head of the Branch and one Enforcement Officer is there to assist him. It has a pivotal role in enforcement activities in the BMC and BDA area in order to counter the stiff challenges faced to check unauthorized constructions both on the Government and private land. It has acquired great significance for grounding of different projects on Government land by removing bottlenecks due to unauthorized constructions over that and protecting it. Enforcement activities of this Wing has been improved by strengthening the Enforcement Squads and organizing their work schedule.

There is a ODA Courts functioning in Enforcement Wing. The OSD (ODA Act) is conducting ODA courts for initiation and disposal of UAP Cases within BDA area.

In the Enforcement Wing of BDA, 14 numbers of Enforcement Squad have been formed and they are deployed in BDA Enforcement Section. They are performing the enforcement activities for removal of road side encroachment of major road in Bhubaneswar City. The Enforcement Squads also attend to the public petitions so as to redress the grievances of public in time. Another segment of enforcement activity is detection of Government land for which all the Enforcement Squads have been instructed to identify Government land under encroachment. A number of patches of Government land have been detected and necessary action has been taken for eviction of encroachments there and protection of the same by construction of boundary wall and fixation of sign board. They are also performing site visits on the basis of direction given in the UAP cases which are subjudice in the court of OSD (ODA Act) BDA. HORTICULTURE DIVISION

**Annual Activities Report of Parks & Plantation (2024–25)**

**1. Development of Bachera Hill Park at Jatani**

Bachera Hill Park at Jatani has been developed as an ecological park over an area of about 30.00 acres. The project, with an estimated cost of ₹8.05 crores, was implemented through DFO Khordha. In the first phase, funds of ₹4.00 crores were sanctioned and utilised for plantation and creation of amenities for park visitors. A mass plantation programme was held on 11.09.2023 with the participation of Hon'ble MLA, Forest Department officials, Municipal staff, and BDA staff. The park now provides extensive green cover and recreational space for the public.

**2. Buddha Hill Top Nature Trail Park**

The Buddha Hill Top Garden has been developed by BDA over an area of 6.00 acres at the hilltop. The project has been completed as a landscaped nature trail park, incorporating lawns, ornamental shrubs, arbours, pathways, garden lighting, and water supply facilities. A nature trail have also been created to promote eco-tourism and provide recreational facilities for the public. Further garden development was integrated along with plantation works, transforming the hilltop into a prominent green and cultural landmark of the city.

**3. Waste to Art Park at K-7, Kalinga Nagar**

The Waste to Art Park at K-7, Kalinga Nagar has been developed by BDA as a theme-based park over 5.00 acres. The park showcases creative installations made from waste materials and includes modern recreational facilities such as a skating rink and Yoga Pindi, along with other Smart Park features (Smart Park 3, 4, 5). Development works have been completed, and the park was formally inaugurated in January 2025 by the Hon'ble Housing & Urban Development Minister along with other distinguished dignitaries. The park is now open for public use and is under regular maintenance, offering an innovative blend of art, environment, health, and leisure.

**4. Maintenance of Existing Parks and Public Amenities**

Apart from creation of new thematic and ecological parks, the Bhubaneswar Development Authority (BDA) continues to look after the regular upkeep and maintenance of major public parks across the city. These include Indira Gandhi Park, Biju Patnaik Park, Madhusudan Das Park, Netaji Subhas Bose Park, Mahatma Gandhi Park, Buddha Jayanti Park, Guru Kelucharan Mohapatra Park, Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Park  along with several other small neighbourhood parks.

The Public- Private Partnership (PPP) Branch functions under the Estate Division of BDA for all projects being taken up under Public Private Partnership mode. Specific work of this branch includes:

• Identify potential real estate projects including premium residential/ commercial and affordable/ slum redevelopment housing projects to be taken up through PPP in BDPA area.

• Coordinate with various departments/ agencies of state government for successful implementation of PPP projects.
• Providing handholding support through PMU, BDA to other project development agencies for structuring of affordable & slum redevelopment housing projects or any other real estate projects being taken on PPP mode.

**A. Affordable/ Slum Redevelopment Housing Projects under PPP Mode:**

As a pioneer organization to achieve the objective of the State Government for Housing for All, Bhubaneswar Development Authority (BDA) has taken up several Affordable & Slum Redevelopment Housing Projects to create a steady supply of affordable housing stocks to cater to the current housing shortage in the EWS segment.

**BHUBANESWAR LAND USE INTELLIGENCE SYSTEM (BLUIS)**

Bhubaneswar Land Use Intelligence System (BLUIS) is an innovate AI based Mobile App (BLUIS) and Portal designed for detecting unauthorized constructions on Govt. lands under the control of GA & PG Department, BMC and BDA. BLUIS is a Geo-tagged repository of all the Govt. lands in Bhubaneswar, created through high resolution satellite imagery to detect all changes taking place on government lands.

[page 84]
BLUIS Cell has been effectively functioning at BDA under the supervision of Sr. Manager (MIS) -cum- Nodal Officer (BLUIS) with effect from 24.06.2020 in accordance with the direction of GA & PG Department, Govt. of Odisha to implement and monitor BLUIS system for safeguarding the Govt. land from encroachment.

The system for detecting unauthorized construction utilizes monthly updated High Definition Satellite imageries integrated with land revenue cadastral maps. The advanced approach employs Artifical Intelligence and Machine Learning techniques to identify land use changes and unauthorised construction effectively. The solution generates comprehensive reports that include alerts, details of land freed from encroachment and pending cases involving officials such as the Ward Officer (WO) Zonal Deputy Commissioner (ZDC), Central Enforcement Monitoring Committee (CEMC).

These reports are then apprised to relevant authorities, like Vice-Chairman, BDA, Commissioner, BMC, Director of Estate, GA & PG Department, Addl. Commissioners (Enforcement) of BDA & BMC, Zonal Deputy Commissioners in the review meetings focused on the eviction of unauthorised encroachments over Govt. Land, facilitating timely enforcement actions and administrative decision-making.

Till 16.08.2025, total alerts generated – 2752 (2161 by Satellite, 281 by citizens and 310 by Departmental officials). Total 1296 no. of encroachments have been removed by the enforcement squads. With the help of this project, government has protected many valuable Govt. land.
