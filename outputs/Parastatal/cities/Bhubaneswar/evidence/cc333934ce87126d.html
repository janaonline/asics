Following are the available discounts: 

  • Below 3 years of age free ride.
  • 100% concession for Totally Blind passenger and 50% Concession to their companion.
  • 100% concession for Mentally Retarded passenger and 50% Concession to their companion.
  • 100% concession for Orthopedically challenged with 50% - 100% body deformity passenger and 50% Concession to their companion.
  • 100% concession for Immune Deficiency (HIV/ AIDS) commuters.
  • 100% concession for 100% hearing impaired persons (deaf & dumb).
  • 50% concession for Orthopedically challenged with 40% - 49% body deformity.
  • 50% concession on Luggage Charges on Non-AC Buses for above categories of Passengers.
